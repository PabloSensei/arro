# Sources and licenses of `ui-kit`

## Icons

Arro's icons are its own set, built from the shapes of Lucide 1.70.0, https://lucide.dev;
`arrowUp`, `arrowDown`, `caseSensitive`, `wholeWord`, `regex` and `minus` come from Lucide
1.43.0, the copy inside the `gpui-kit-assets` 0.9.0 crate, under the same ISC license.
They are generated by `cargo run -p xtask -- icons --lucide <DIR>` from the table in
`tools/xtask/src/icons.rs`: size 16 or 20, a stroke width of 1.5 in viewBox-24 units,
colors from the light and dark palettes of the theme. The symbol-kind and status icons
are drawn for Arro, with no text or fonts inside the SVG.

The Lucide license (ISC), with the MIT part inherited from Feather, sits next to the icons
in `assets/icons/LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt`.

Where the colors and metrics come from is described in `crates/editor/theme/NOTICE.md`.
